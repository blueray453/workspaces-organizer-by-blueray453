Move a file to another window

Drag the file. Move to the right edge to browse apps. Hover an app in the right panel to see its windows in a second strip. When the bottom indicator overflows, windows from the same app are grouped within each workspace with a number badge. Click a grouped icon to open the full window selector for just that group; during a drag, hover the group to open the anchored flyout instead. Hover a window to preview it, then drop on a highlighted zone. Hold above or below either list to scroll: the farther out, the faster.
